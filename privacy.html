Rakaposhica Dashboard

Privacy policy

Last updated November 4, 2026

Rakaposhica Dashboard is a web app for making sell sheets, emailing them to your customers and keeping track of replies, leads, inventory and warehouse stock. This page explains what it reads, what it keeps and where.

The short version

  • Everything you make is kept in your own browser, in your own Google Drive or Microsoft OneDrive, and, if your company uses the Company feature, in your company's own Cloudflare account. There is no central server where everyone's data is pooled.
  • Emails are sent from your own mailbox, only when you press Send.
  • Gmail is read only to notice replies from people you emailed from the app, and to let you pick emails from your Sent folder to track.
  • No ads, no tracking or analytics, and your data is never sold or shared for advertising.

Signing in

Signing in is optional. When you sign in with Google or Microsoft, the app receives your name and email address so it can show who is signed in, keep your data under your account, and (in a company) show coworkers who owns a customer.

What the app can do with your Google account

PermissionWhat it's used for
Send email on your behalf (gmail.send)Sends the sell sheets and emails you write, to the people you choose, when you press Send.
Read your email (gmail.readonly)Checks for replies from people you emailed from the app, so they can be marked "Replied", and lets you choose emails from your Sent folder to track. Other emails are not stored or shown.

See or edit your Drive files (drive.readonly, drive)Asked for only when you use these features: opening a sell sheet from Drive, the Inventory folder of sell sheets, saving into a folder you pick, and keeping an existing Google Sheet in step with the rack map. The app only opens the files and folders you choose.

What the app can do with your Microsoft account

User.Read (your name and email), Mail.Send (sends the emails you write when you press Send) and Files.ReadWrite.AppFolder (a private app folder in your OneDrive for the same sync copy described above). offline_access keeps you signed in.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Rakaposhica Dashboard's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the features described on this page. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not used to train AI or machine-learning models, and not read by people except when you ask for help and share it yourself, or where the law requires.

Where your data is kept

  • Your browser. Customers, sell sheets, the Sent list, leads, notes, to-dos, warehouse stock and settings are stored in your browser's storage on each device.
  • Your Google Drive or OneDrive (when you're signed in), in the app's private folder, so your devices stay in step.
  • The sign-in helper. To keep you signed in, your company can run a small helper on its own Cloudflare account. It exchanges Google's sign-in codes for access, and the long-lived sign-in it returns is encrypted and kept in your browser. The helper does not see or keep your emails or files.
  • Company (optional). If your company turns on the Company feature, the customers, leads, Sent history, notes, to-dos, email templates and rack map it chooses to share are stored in a database in your company's Cloudflare account, visible to the people your company admin invites.
  • Google Sheet (optional). If you link a Google Sheet to the rack map, the rack map is written to that sheet in your Google Drive.

Sharing

Your data goes only where you send it: emails go to the recipients you pick, shared company data goes to your coworkers, and a linked Google Sheet goes to whoever you share it with in Google Sheets. The app uses Google, Microsoft and Cloudflare to run; their own privacy policies apply to their services.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Sign out from the account menu to stop the app using your Google or Microsoft account on that device.
  • Remove access at any time: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, Microsoft at account.live.com/consent/Manage.
  • Delete what's on a device by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. Settings → Backup & reset lets you download a copy first.
  • Delete the synced copy: in Google Drive, go to Settings → Manage apps → Rakaposhica Dashboard → Delete hidden app data.
  • Company data: leave the company in Settings → Company, or ask your company admin to remove you. When the last person leaves, the company's shared data is deleted.

Children

The app is a business tool and isn't meant for children under 13.
